Document conventions

“Customer” means an ordering user. “Partner” means a restaurant owner or invited restaurant team member. “Admin” means a Halal Delivery platform operator. Monetary examples use euros and cents.

Halal Delivery Functional Specification2

01 · Product overview

Marketplace and operating roles

Halal Delivery is a multi-sided food-delivery marketplace POC. Customers discover verified halal restaurants and groceries, configure menu items, receive automatic offers, select an optional charity impact allocation and place a simulated paid order. Partners manage their storefront and campaigns. Platform operators govern catalogue changes and reconcile commercial liabilities.

Public visitor

  • Views the marketing landing page and social-impact proposition.
  • Searches and filters restaurants and grocery stores.
  • Views menus, fees, reviews and active offers.
  • Can register a customer account or sign in.

Customer

  • Configures products and maintains a restaurant-specific basket.
  • Receives the best eligible automatic promotion.
  • Chooses optional impact support and demo payment method.
  • Views immediate and scheduled orders and reviews confirmed purchases.

Restaurant partner

  • Views operational status and pending submissions.
  • Creates categories and submits governed menu/profile changes.
  • Publishes announcements and automatic promotions.
  • Invites additional restaurant team members.

Platform administrator

  • Approves menu and profile submissions.
  • Invites new restaurant owners.
  • Manages restaurant commission percentages.
  • Reviews monthly sales, liabilities, donations and audit detail.

02 · Access and sessions

Authentication and POC role testing

The login experience supports manual email/password authentication, customer self-registration and an optional Google Identity integration. Sessions are stored in secure host cookies and expire automatically.

Public POC testing hub

On a non-loopback development or ngrok host, the login page displays explicit Customer, Admin and Restaurant role buttons. Each button stays on the current public origin, selects a pocRole query parameter and prefills the corresponding demo credentials.

Public-session rule. A public ngrok hostname has one cookie session. Testers must sign out before changing roles and must test roles sequentially.

Local isolated testing

Local development preserves independent sessions by using three loopback hosts:

RoleLocal URLDemo account
Customerhttp://127.0.0.1:5173/logincustomer@halaldelivery.demo
Administratorhttp://127.0.0.2:5173/loginadmin@halaldelivery.demo
Restaurant ownerhttp://127.0.0.3:5173/loginowner@emberandolive.demo

The testing panel and credential prefill are automatically available in development. A production build hides them unless VITE_ENABLE_POC_TESTING=true is explicitly enabled.

03 · Public experience

Landing page and marketplace proposition

The public landing page introduces the marketplace, requests a delivery location and explains how every order can create community impact. The page provides direct navigation to restaurant discovery, the impact story, supported causes, customer login and partner access.

Customer proposition

  • Verified halal marketplace positioning.
  • Delivery-location entry and restaurant discovery call to action.
  • Restaurant cards and direct menu navigation.
  • English and Dutch language controls.

Impact proposition

  • Explains that donations come from platform commission.
  • Profiles participating charitable organisations.
  • Allows customers to select up to three causes at checkout.
  • Keeps donations separate from restaurant payable amounts.

04 · Discovery

Restaurant and grocery discovery

The discovery screen supports delivery and collection modes, restaurant and grocery business types, address entry, search, sorting and multi-faceted filtering. The corrected brand container keeps the supplied portrait logo fully visible and centred.

Available discovery controls

Find and refine

  • Search by restaurant, shop, cuisine or grocery category.
  • Switch between restaurants and groceries.
  • Filter free delivery, favourites, rating, opening status and offers.
  • Filter individual cuisines through checkboxes or visual shortcuts.

Compare places

  • Sort by best match, delivery speed or rating.
  • View halal verification status and closed status.
  • Compare delivery estimate, delivery fee and free-delivery threshold.
  • Favourite and revisit selected restaurants in the current session.

05 · Restaurant storefront

Menu, restaurant information and offers

Each restaurant has a dedicated branded storefront. The hero presents verification, description, rating, delivery estimate, opening hours and the current offer. Customers can open reviews, restaurant information, offer details and a clear fee explanation. The restaurant name, address and professional-trader responsibility statement are repeated in a business-details footer.

Menu behaviour

  • Search across item names and descriptions.
  • Jump between menu categories and browse responsive product cards.
  • Show popularity, offer, vegetarian and availability labels.
  • View ingredients, declared allergens, VAT and availability details.
  • Keep deactivated items visible but dimmed and prevent them from being ordered.
  • When an active restaurant is closed, explain that customers can preorder and select a delivery slot for its next opening day.
  • Recommend a supplementary item after products are added.

05 · Configurable products

Modifiers and customer item notes

Products such as pizzas, burgers, kebabs, meals, drinks and milkshakes can use reusable modifier templates. Required and optional groups are rendered as accessible radio-button or checkbox selections, with extra-price adjustments calculated into the line price.

Configuration rules

  • Required modifier groups must be completed before the product can be added.
  • Multiple-choice groups enforce configured maximum selection counts.
  • Price deltas are shown per option and included in basket/checkout totals.
  • The same product with different options or notes remains a separate basket line.
  • Customers may add a free-text instruction of up to 300 characters.
  • Item notes are explicitly not accepted as allergy declarations.

06 · Promotions

Automatic offer selection

The basket previews eligible automatic promotions as items are added. Quantity campaigns can prompt the customer to add another qualifying item. Announcements are displayed as messages only and never change totals.

One-winner stacking policy. The engine evaluates every eligible active order-value and quantity offer, applies only the offer with the greatest customer saving, includes delivery savings in the comparison and resolves equal savings deterministically by promotion ID.

Supported promotion types

TypeRule optionsFinancial effect
Promotional announcementTitle, message, enabled state and campaign datesNone
Order-value offerPercentage, fixed euro amount or free delivery; optional minimum food orderDiscounts food or delivery
Quantity offerBuy/reward quantities, reward percentage and qualifying/reward scopesDiscounts eligible item units

06 · Checkout

Order review, impact and payment simulation

The checkout drawer consolidates order review, the optional impact choice and payment selection in one flow. It shows the selected promotion, exact savings, delivery and service fees, and the final server-payable amount.

Authoritative checkout sequence

1. Send item IDs, quantities, modifiers and note snapshots
›
2. Reload menu prices and active rules on the server
›
3. Select one best automatic offer
›
4. Reconcile payment and settlement totals
  • Basket savings are previews only; the server never trusts a client-provided saving.
  • Percentage offers are rounded deterministically and fixed discounts cannot reduce food subtotal below zero.
  • Free-delivery offers zero the actual otherwise-payable delivery fee.
  • Service fee, commission, payment fee, donation basis, restaurant payable and platform fee are recomputed from discounted values.
  • Credit card and iDEAL/Wero are demonstration choices; no real payment credentials are processed.
  • A successful POC payment creates an order, order-item snapshots, promotion snapshot and confirmation-email simulation.

07 · Customer account

Order history, receipts and reviews

Signed-in customers can open their account to see confirmed orders. Each record retains restaurant and item details, gross payment, donation amount, email status and the applied promotion title and savings.

Review eligibility

  • Only the customer who placed the order may submit its review.
  • The order must be confirmed and its confirmation email must be recorded as sent.
  • A customer can review each eligible order once and can update that review.
  • Verified-order ratings contribute to restaurant ratings shown in discovery and the storefront.

08 · Partner centre

Restaurant operational overview

The partner centre is a role-protected workspace with five sections: Overview, Menu manager, Restaurant profile, Offers and Team members. The overview gives a concise operational status and routes the owner to common tasks.

Overview information

  • Published item count and category count.
  • Number of catalogue or profile submissions awaiting admin review.
  • Halal/profile verification status.
  • Recent submissions with category, price and workflow status.
  • Customer-facing restaurant profile preview.
Governance model. Catalogue and profile submissions are stored separately from live customer content. They go live only after an administrator approves them.

09 · Menu governance

Categories and menu submissions

Partners can create and rename menu categories immediately for use inside the workspace. New menu items enter a governed review workflow and do not overwrite the published customer menu. Published items also have an immediate operational Activate/Deactivate control, independent of content approval.

Menu item data

Commercial and display

  • Name, category and useful description.
  • Price including VAT and a 9% or 21% VAT classification.
  • Availability window, vegetarian flag and landscape image URL.
  • Customer preview before submission.

Food safety and configuration

  • Ingredient declaration and EU 14 allergen selection.
  • Standard or configurable item type.
  • Reusable modifier template with editable groups/options.
  • Required/default options and bounded extra prices.
Immediate availability control. Deactivation does not delete or rewrite approved catalogue content. The item remains visible to customers with an “Unavailable” state, while add-to-basket and authoritative checkout validation both block it.

09 · Profile governance

Restaurant profile and checkout settings

Partners can propose updates to the customer-facing brand, address, operating hours and fee settings. Submitted changes follow the same approval trail as menu items.

Managed profile fields

  • Trading name, description and restaurant address.
  • Logo URL and storefront landing image URL.
  • Opening and closing times.
  • Minimum food-order amount and delivery fee.
  • Optional free-delivery threshold.
  • Percentage service fee and maximum service-fee cap.

Approved values are shown to customers and snapshotted at checkout so historical orders remain explainable even after profile settings change.

10 · Campaigns

Promotion guidance and campaign calendar

The Offers workspace contains a built-in guide, worked examples, a campaign editor and a calendar of existing campaigns. Promotions publish directly for the POC and are visible only when enabled and within their configured start/end window.

Message-only announcements

A promotional announcement is a distinct campaign type for scheduled storefront messages such as “Something special is coming this weekend”. The interface explicitly states that announcements never affect basket or checkout totals. Legacy manual order_offer records are treated as announcements for backward compatibility.

10 · Automatic order-value offers

Percentage, fixed amount and free delivery

Partners can create automatic order-value campaigns using explicit, validated financial rules. An optional minimum is always evaluated against the eligible pre-discount food subtotal.

RuleValidationCalculation
Percentage off foodWhole percentage from 1 to 100Percentage of eligible food subtotal, deterministically rounded
Fixed euro discountPositive value, bounded to €1,000 in portal inputFixed cents capped at the food subtotal
Free deliveryNo discount value; optional non-negative minimumRemoves the delivery fee that would otherwise be charged

Quantity-offer configuration

Quantity offers provide buy-one-get-one, cheapest-free, second-item-half-price and custom presets. Partners configure buy quantity, reward quantity, reward percentage and qualifying/reward scope across the whole menu, selected categories or selected items.

Team-member invitations

Restaurant owners can invite additional workspace users through a secure, single-use link. The invitation is tied to the restaurant and intended role, expires after seven days and requires the invited user to choose a password.

Invitation flow

Owner enters team email
›
Server issues a single-use token
›
Owner shares generated link
›
Invitee creates account and enters portal
  • The generated link can be copied from the workspace.
  • Existing and invited team members are visible to the owner.
  • The invited account is linked to the correct restaurant before portal redirection.

12 · Platform administration

Approval centre and commercial terms

The admin workspace presents pending catalogue and profile work alongside restaurant onboarding and commercial controls. Platform operators can review validated menu submissions and publish them to the customer storefront.

Administrative functions

Governance

  • Search and approve menu submissions.
  • Approve customer-facing profile changes.
  • See allergens, VAT and price before publication.
  • Refresh the approval queue on demand.

Commercial operations

  • Create seven-day restaurant-owner invitations.
  • Set commission percentage per restaurant.
  • See pending and sent invitation counts.
  • Reset deterministic POC data only after explicit confirmation.
POC reset protection. Reset is deliberately destructive to POC-tagged data and requires a browser confirmation plus the server confirmation phrase. It is not part of normal operations.

13 · Finance and impact

Monthly settlement report

The monthly report turns paid-order snapshots into a traceable operational ledger. It distinguishes cash received from liabilities and platform revenue, and can be printed for settlement review.

Report contents

  • Paid-order count and gross sales received.
  • Total customer promotion savings, including delivery savings.
  • Restaurant payable, charity payable, contracted commission and payment fees.
  • Net amount retained after donations and payment fees.
  • Charity settlement schedule with contributing-order count and payout status.
  • Restaurant sales split by order count, agreed rate, gross, commission and amount owed.
  • Order-level audit trail with promotion snapshot and donation allocation.

14 · Financial controls

Settlement and reconciliation rules

Every confirmed order stores enough immutable information to explain the customer payment and downstream settlement. The API rejects a checkout if its financial invariants do not reconcile.

Core reconciliation

ValueRule
Original food subtotalSum of authoritative menu prices, modifier deltas and quantities before promotion
Discounted food subtotalOriginal food subtotal less the selected food-promotion saving, never below zero
Delivery payableConfigured delivery fee less free-delivery saving, never below zero
Gross/payment amountDiscounted food subtotal + delivery payable + capped service fee
CommissionRestaurant agreement percentage applied to discounted food subtotal
Restaurant payableDiscounted food subtotal + delivery payable − commission
Platform feeCommission + service fee
Donation basisOptional share of platform commission; never deducted from restaurant payable
Payment feeCalculated from the discounted gross/payment amount

Stored order snapshot

  • Original and discounted food subtotals.
  • Food-promotion and delivery-discount amounts.
  • Applied promotion ID, title and type.
  • Delivery fee, service fee, gross paid, commission and payment fee.
  • Restaurant payable, platform fee and donation allocations.
  • Item names, quantities, configured option summaries and customer notes.
Future payment integration. A Stripe PaymentIntent or other real payment request must use the server-reconciled gross amount. The client basket remains a preview and must never define the amount charged.

14 · Controls and quality

Validation, security and compatibility

Role and session controls

  • API paths enforce customer, partner or admin role requirements.
  • Password hashes use Argon2.
  • Session cookies are host-only and expire automatically.
  • Invitation tokens are single use and time limited.

Promotion validation

  • Percentage must be 1–100.
  • Fixed cents must be positive and bounded.
  • Minimum order must be non-negative.
  • Dates must be valid and end after start.
  • Malformed type/rule combinations are rejected.

Catalogue validation

  • Required names, descriptions, price, VAT and image URL.
  • Ingredient and allergen review data.
  • Bounded modifier groups, options, defaults and price deltas.
  • Approval isolation between submission and published record.

Backward compatibility

  • Legacy order_offer rows continue to load.
  • Legacy manual rows map to message-only announcements.
  • Database additions use compatible schema evolution.
  • Historical order snapshots do not depend on current rules.

Language and responsive behaviour

The customer-facing landing, login, storefront, basket and checkout expose English/Dutch controls. Major customer screens adapt to desktop and mobile layouts. Interactive controls use labels, semantic buttons and visible focus/selection states; modal surfaces identify themselves as dialogs.
